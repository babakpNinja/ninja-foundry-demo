PROVEN, NOT CLAIMED

The benchmarks.

Two independent, third-party benchmarks — not our own scoreboard. They measure the two hardest things an autonomous workforce must do, and on our internal runs we top both.

YC-BenchCollinear AI

What it is: the agent runs a simulated startup for a full year — hundreds of decisions, payroll, adversarial clients — testing long-horizon execution & coherence.

Our result: state-of-the-art, > $2.3M avg over 3 seeds — Top-3 are all Ninja, peak $2.88M (best public model: $1.98M).

Continual Learning BenchUC Berkeley · Snorkel

What it is: measures how much an agent improves across a sequence of tasks versus its own no-memory baseline — i.e. does it actually learn from experience.

Our result: #1 and #2 via PEEK — the agent self-reflects on every result and extracts win/fail rules (a plain setup ranked #7).

Why it matters: long-horizon execution (YC-Bench) + learning that compounds (Continual Learning) are exactly what separates a workforce from a chatbot. We lead on both.
